Our Story

Crafted with

At Layali, we believe that the best moments in life are sweetened by handcrafted treats. Born from a love for artisan desserts and specialty drinks, we bring you a curated selection of frozen yogurt, bubble waffles, canned cakes, and signature beverages — each made fresh, with premium ingredients.

Whether you're craving a rich pistachio milkshake, a refreshing mango iced tea, or our famous canned cheesecake, every item on our menu is designed to delight your senses and create moments worth savoring.
